The Best Movie According to Praveen

The top 3 movies of all-time.

Vaaranam Aayiram (வாரணம் ஆயிரம்):

Surya, an NSG official, is on a mission to rescue someone when he gets the news of his father's demise. He starts reminiscing about the bond he shared with his father and the stories related to him.

Good Bad Ugly:

After several years in prison, a former gangster seeks a quiet life with his family. But when his son winds up in jail, he must return to his old ways.

Leo:

Violently pursued by criminals who insist he's a former gangster, a humble cafe owner fights to shield his family — and the truth about his identity.
